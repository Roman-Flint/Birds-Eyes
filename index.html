How booking works

Nothing is booked or paid for until the details and the final price are confirmed with you by email.

  1. Send a request

    Pick a package, add the address and see your estimate straight away.

  2. Get a confirmation

    Once the weather and airspace rules for your area are checked, you get an email confirming the time and final price.

  3. Shoot day

    Be there, or let the shoot go ahead without you, for example while you’re at work.

  4. Delivery

    Photos and video arrive by download link. Prints take 1–2 weeks.
