Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Birds-Eyes Drone Services is a drone photography business in Auckland, New Zealand. We look after your personal information in line with the Privacy Act 2020. If you have a question about your information, email birdseyesnz@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you send a quote request, we collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number
  • the address of the property and its suburb
  • the job details you choose: the package, property type, preferred date and time, print options and any notes

We don’t use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we don’t collect payment details on this website.

Why we collect it

We only use your information to give you a quote, check the airspace and the rules for your address, arrange the shoot, deliver your photos and video, and contact you about the job. We don’t sell it, and we don’t send marketing emails.

Services that handle your information

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers the quote form to our email. Your form details pass through its servers, which may be outside New Zealand.
  • Gmail (Google) stores our emails, including your quote request.
  • OpenStreetMap address search (Nominatim and Photon). As you type an address in the quote form, the text you type is sent to these services to suggest matching addresses and check whether the address is in our service area.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this website, so Google can see your IP address when a page loads.

Photos and video of your property

The photos and video we take are for you. We won’t use them in our gallery, on social media or anywhere else without your permission. We try to keep neighbouring properties and people out of shots, and we delete footage you don’t need.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and job details for as long as we need them to do the job, answer questions about it and meet our tax record-keeping obligations. We keep your original photo and video files for about 3 months after delivery so you can ask for another copy, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. You can also ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it by law. Email birdseyesnz@gmail.com and we’ll reply within 20 working days.

If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
